###### Your Personal Relationship Manager

---

Mash is a PRM — a Personal Relationship Manager. An AI coworker for the network
behind your deals, your career, your people.

Tell it what matters as it happens, and it remembers. It finds the rest in the
tools you already use, watches the world for what changed, and hands it back when
it counts: a verified brief before every meeting, a reminder of what you promised
last time, a nudge the moment someone is owed a thank-you, a gift or a call.

Not the company's CRM. Yours.

**What we work on** — capturing spoken context in seconds and turning it into
structure; resolving people and companies across years of email, calls and notes;
keeping a relationship graph correct as the world changes; retrieval where being
plausible isn't good enough; and agents that have to judge when it's worth
interrupting you.

Built in Toronto by a team that has shipped AI at Google, Amazon, Ada and Zowie.
Backed by Castle Island, Whitecap, Maple VC, Strategic Cyber Ventures, SpaceCadet,
and angels including Sunny Madra and Balaji Srinivasan.

Most of our work lives in private repos. What's public here is the odd fork and
anything we open-source along the way.

We're hiring engineers who want small scope-to-impact ratios and no layers.
